Joseph Stalin’s Paranoid Purge

Joseph Stalin purged his officer corps of thousands of talented leaders before the start of World War II. It almost cost Russia the war.

  • Authors: John W. Osborn, Jr.
  • Published: June, 2009
  • Source

Officer corps expect to suffer heavy losses in war. The entire1914 class of France’sSt. Cyr military academy, for example, perished inWorld War I. But no officer corps ever suffered the degree of loss that theRed Armysuffered in peacetime, at the hands of its own government, during dictatorJoseph Stalin’sparanoidpurgeof1937-1938.
